# Hospitality POS SVG Prototype

These are editable vector mockups of the main Hospitality POS screens. Each SVG is a separate 1440 × 900 artboard and can be imported into Adobe XD using **File → Import** or by dragging the SVG onto an XD canvas.

## Artboards

1. `01-login.svg` — Login and practice account selection
2. `02-dashboard.svg` — Restaurant and hotel operations overview
3. `03-orders.svg` — Waiter order entry and order/payment status
4. `04-food-inventory.svg` — Food Storekeeper product and stock management
5. `05-beverage-inventory.svg` — Beverage Storekeeper inventory, including beer
6. `06-kitchen.svg` — Kitchen order board and supply requests
7. `07-accountant-approvals.svg` — Accountant stock-request approvals and payment confirmation
8. `08-manager-team.svg` — Employee creation and role management
9. `09-housekeeping.svg` — Hotel room status and housekeeping work
10. `10-reports.svg` — Sales, payments and stock reports
11. `11-settings.svg` — Profile, theme and training workspace settings

The artboards are static visual prototypes. They do not contain native Adobe XD wires or interactive components.
